Cattie's World 2

As we've all clearly experienced, Cattie's World is probably one of the most glitchy, unoptimised games ever. After every update, it becomes laggier and laggier to the point that you cannot even run it normally on a decent computer, LET ALONE a mobile phone! And that while I was striving for it to be fully compatible with touchscreens.

But, as which might be clear to some of the more technical players, the code of the game... it is really, really messy. No seriously, have a look at the code! (yes, you can!)

Another limitation of the Cattie's World engine, is that everything is hardcoded. A ton of stuff, breaks com-plete-ly after only changing one ini mini teeni tiny thing. E.g. adding a new vehicle, or even changing some values can BREAK EVERYTHING. Add a vehicle? Want to torture yourself for 5+ hours trying to change all the object IDs in the code and probably forgetting a couple causing it to not work and make you say go fuck yourself and undo all that work? While this sounds pretty bad and, quite frankly, somewhat serious, don't fear, my kid , as I have the golden solution to this very problem, that has been an option for all those 6+ months I've worked on it, and I never realised it was there until now.

Teleporting to the shadow realm by crushing your spine? Having parts of your blood float in literal air? Having your universe run at a slowly but surely 69 minutes per frame? Well, with the NEW and improved CopyCat Engine that's all over.
In all seriousness, the new "CopyCat" engine is what i called the engine i made that runs at the core of the game, which is basically a full rewrite of the entire game, focusing on pure performance, stability and being better in every way. it runs at a sweet sweet 150 fps in a decently filled level (as of now there's only building blocks no things like saws and stuff that would lag more). My goal is trying to have the game run at >60 fps on my old phone, and >120 on my PC, ensuring good performance on any computer. The game will (hopefully) be released on steam for probably something around 1 - 3 bucks (Since steam charges 100 bucks for uploading a single game I need a way to earn it back) Oh yeah and since I made this website back when I was absolutely dreadful at html I might even redo the entire site design!
